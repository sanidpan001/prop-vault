What is LTV in Loan Against Property?

LTV means Loan to Value Ratio. RBI allows banks to give max 70% of market value for residential property, 60% for commercial, and 50% for land. For example, if your flat market value is ₹1 Crore, bank will give max ₹60-70 Lakh based on property type. But bank also checks income. Even if property is ₹1 Cr, if your income is low, you may get only ₹40 Lakh. PropVault calculator shows both calculations side by side so you know real reason for eligibility.

How to Increase LAP Eligibility?

  • Show Rental Income: If property gives ₹30k rent, add to income - eligibility increases by ₹15 Lakh.
  • Add Co-Applicant: Add spouse/child income - banks allow 2-3 co-applicants for LAP.
  • Reduce LTV Demand: Ask for 50% LTV instead of 70% - banks approve faster and give lower interest 0.5% less.
  • Clear Existing Loans: A ₹20k personal loan EMI reduces LAP eligibility by ₹18 Lakh. Close it before LAP.
  • Property Valuation: Get valuation from 2 banks - one may value your property 10% higher = ₹6 Lakh more loan.
